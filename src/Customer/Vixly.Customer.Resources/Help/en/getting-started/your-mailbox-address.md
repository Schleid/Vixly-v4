Your mailbox address is the delivery address that {brandName} gives you for online purchases. It is not your home address or the address of this website. Your customer code identifies your packages when they reach the warehouse.

New here? The optional tour introduces the mailbox, package announcement and shipment steps after profile setup. Open Help and choose **Take the guided tour** to replay it.

## Steps
1. Sign in and open **My mailbox**. Find **Your mailbox address**.
2. Read your customer code and the complete address shown below it.
3. At the shop’s checkout, use that address as the shipping or delivery address. Copy the addressee, street, extra address line, city, region, postal code and country exactly as displayed. Keep your customer code wherever it appears in those lines.
4. Check the final delivery address before paying the shop. Do not replace the warehouse city with your home city. The shop’s billing address is a separate question.
5. When the shop or carrier gives you the package tracking number, announce the package in your account.

## What happens next
If no mailbox address appears, contact {brandName} before ordering. Do not choose another warehouse or invent an address. Delivering your purchase to the mailbox is the first journey; creating a shipment tells the company where to send it next.

## On this screen
1. Your customer code identifies your account.
2. This is the complete address to copy at the shop’s checkout.
