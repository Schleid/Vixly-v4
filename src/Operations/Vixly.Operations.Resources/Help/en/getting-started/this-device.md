Working for {brandName} can require an approved device as well as your own staff sign-in. A successful password check does not by itself mean that a new computer is approved for work.

Read the device page shown after sign-in. It explains whether this computer needs registration, is waiting for a decision or cannot currently enter the workspace. Keep the device with you while following its instructions.

## Steps
1. Sign in with your own account on the computer you intend to use.
2. If the device-registration page appears, enter **Name of this computer** with a name your company can recognize. Select the appropriate device type shown on the form.
3. Choose **Ask to add this computer** and read the next page. If approval is pending, wait for the company’s decision rather than repeatedly registering the same computer.
4. Return to the workspace when the device page allows you to continue. Use **Sign out** when you finish a shared workstation session.

## What happens next
An approved computer still requires the appropriate staff sign-in. Signing out does not remove its device approval. If the device is blocked, lost or no longer yours to use, contact the person responsible for device support. Do not share an account or use another person’s sign-in to get past the message.
