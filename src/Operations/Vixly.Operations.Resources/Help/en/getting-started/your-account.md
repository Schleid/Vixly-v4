Use your account page to maintain the sign-in you use for {brandName}. These choices concern your own password, language and sessions. They do not change another person’s account or the company’s settings.

Have a safe place ready before replacing recovery codes. Keep passwords, codes and sign-in links private, including when someone is helping you with an account problem.

## Steps
1. Open **My account** and check that the name and email are yours.
2. Select **Language of my pages and emails** and choose **Save** to change the language of your pages and emails.
3. Under **Password**, enter **Current password** and **New password (12 characters or more)**, then choose **Change my password**. Read the result before leaving the page.
4. If recovery codes are available, choose **Make new recovery codes** to replace them. Complete identity confirmation when requested and keep the new set securely.
5. Review **Where I am signed in**. Use **Sign out** beside another session to sign it out when appropriate.

## What happens next
Replacement recovery codes make the old set unusable and are shown only once. Ending another session does not approve or revoke that computer as a staff device. If you do not recognize a session, tell the person responsible for account support at your company.
