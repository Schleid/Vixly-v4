Your workspace brings together the work you do for {brandName}. Use the navigation to reach the tasks available to your account. Your starting page can show work waiting, useful shortcuts and setup tasks for the people who manage the company.

The customer portal at {portalAddress} is a separate place for customers to follow their own business with the company. Continue staff work from this workspace rather than treating the customer portal as another staff menu.

## Steps
1. Open **Home** to return to your starting page. Check the company name before entering information.
2. Choose a task from the navigation or an available shortcut. The choices depend on your work responsibilities.
3. Read any waiting-work counters shown on the page. Open the relevant counter to reach the list behind it; a count is not itself a completed task.
4. Open **Help** for instructions, or use the question-mark link in the header for help with your current page.

## What happens next
Opening a page does not complete the work listed there. Follow the instructions in the relevant task screen and check its result. Keep your account private and sign out when you finish on a shared workstation.
