When you work from another country, {brandName} may ask you to sign in again or complete an additional account check. Read the message shown on the current page rather than assuming that your previous session still applies.

A device can also be blocked from entering the workspace. An account confirmation and a device decision are different things. Completing one does not promise that the other restriction has been removed.

## Steps
1. Return to your company’s staff sign-in page and use your own account. Check the company name before entering information.
2. If the page asks for an authenticator code, use your own authenticator and follow the confirmation instructions. If it asks for your password, use that requested check instead.
3. Read the result. If the device is blocked or cannot continue, contact the person responsible for device support at the company and explain the message you saw.
4. Continue only when the page allows it. Keep recovery codes and account links private while asking for help.

## What happens next
You may return to the workspace after the requested checks, or need company assistance with the device. Do not register repeated replacement devices, share another person’s account or promise a customer that access has been restored before you can actually enter your task screen.
