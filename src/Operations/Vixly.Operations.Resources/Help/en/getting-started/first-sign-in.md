Your invitation gives you access to work for {brandName}. Use the link sent to you and create your own sign-in. Keep that link private: it is intended for the invited person.

The steps shown depend on the company’s sign-in requirements and whether this device is ready for staff work. Complete the requested account checks before expecting to reach the workspace.

## Steps
1. Open your invitation link and check that it belongs to the company you expect. Read the page before entering a password.
2. Enter **New password** and choose **Continue**. Use a password you can keep private.
3. If you are asked to set up an authenticator, follow the displayed setup instructions. Enter the code from your own authenticator and choose **Confirm**.
4. Store any recovery codes shown before continuing. If the next page asks to register this device or wait for approval, follow that page’s instructions.

## What happens next
After the required sign-in and device steps, you reach the staff workspace. You may see only the tasks assigned to your account. An invitation does not mean every company task is available to you. If the link has ended or does not belong to you, contact the person who invited you rather than using a colleague’s account.
