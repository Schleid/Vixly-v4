Your website shows {brandName}'s own name, logo, colours, texts, services and locations. You change a draft; visitors see nothing new until you publish.

## Choose a theme
1. Open **Website** in the menu.
2. Under **Theme and font**, read the short description of each theme. **Classic** is the standard page, **Horizon** opens dark and bold with a route drawing, **Air & Sea** is bright with a large photo and air and ocean side by side.
3. Use *Preview: Horizon* (or another theme) at the top to see your draft in that theme, in a new tab. Nothing is saved.
4. Pick the theme and a font, then select **Save**.
5. Select **Publish now** when the preview looks right.

## Air and sea photos
The Air & Sea theme shows your own air cargo and ocean cargo photos. Upload them under **Images**. Until you do, a simple illustration in your colours is shown. Use your own photos, or photos you have the right to use.

## Plans
Themes, fonts and the air and sea photos come with the plans that include website themes. On other plans you can still preview every theme. If your plan changes, your published website stays exactly as it is; the theme and font are locked in the editor until you change plans.

## Good to know
- Sign-in, account creation, tracking, languages and the footer links are always on the page, in every theme.
- Key figures (services, countries, locations) are counted from what you set up; nothing is invented.
- Check the preview in English, French and Spanish: longer texts may wrap differently.
