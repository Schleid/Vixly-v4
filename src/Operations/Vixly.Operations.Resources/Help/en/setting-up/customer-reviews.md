Customers who have used your service can review {brandName} in the customer portal. You decide, review by review, what appears on your website. Nothing is published until you publish it, and you never change a customer's words.

## Before you start
Your plan includes the customer portal.

## Steps
1. Open *Your website* and choose **Customer testimonials**.
2. Tick **Ask customers for reviews in the customer portal** and choose **Save**. Customers whose shipment was delivered or picked up now see an invitation on their home page and a review page in the menu.
3. Choose **New** to see the reviews waiting for you. Each one shows the stars, the words, the name as the customer chose to show it (such as "Marie P.") and the date.
4. To show a review on your website, choose **Publish**. A customer who did not agree to be quoted cannot be published: their review is private feedback for {brandName}.
5. To keep a review off the website, choose a reason in **Reason (optional)** and choose **Hide**. The customer never sees the reason.
6. Use the arrows to change the order of published reviews. Up to six appear on the home page.
7. For a poor experience, choose **Open a ticket**. Customer service receives a ticket about the customer's last finished shipment.

## What happens next
- A customer can change their review at any time. It comes back under **New** and leaves the website until you publish it again.
- A customer can delete their review. It leaves the website at once.
- Turning the request off hides the portal page. Reviews already sent stay in your list.

## Good to know
- Choose reviews honestly. U.S. rules on reviews forbid hiding negative reviews while suggesting the ones shown are all of them. Your home page shows them as "What our customers say", with no average rating and no count, so it never makes that claim.
- Record a reason when you hide a review. Hide one for the same reasons whatever its stars: personal information, abusive words, not about your service, or a suspected fake.
