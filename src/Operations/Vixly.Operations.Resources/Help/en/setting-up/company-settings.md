Use company settings to review how {brandName} records money, measurements and daily work. These choices affect more than one screen, so check the current values before changing them. Some readers can review settings without changing them; follow the editable controls and save button shown on your page.

## Steps
1. In the Settings part of the menu, open **General settings**.
2. Review **Billing currency**. Read the notice beside it: once invoices exist, the currency is locked.
3. Check **Weight**, **Dimensions** and **Time zone** against the way your company works.
4. Review the collection, credit, public pricing and delivery choices. Read the sign-in requirements before changing them.
5. Review the document prefixes under **Numbering**. If the page allows editing, choose **Save**.

## What happens next
Read the result and reopen the page to confirm the recorded choices. A saved setting does not itself create an invoice, receive a package or configure a payment provider. Use the dedicated invoice and payment settings pages for their detailed rules.
