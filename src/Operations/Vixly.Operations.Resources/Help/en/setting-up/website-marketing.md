The **Air & Sea** theme turns the {brandName} home page into a full sales page. Every part shows only when you give it real content, so nothing is ever invented.

## What you fill in (Your website > texts)
- **Small line above the headline** and promises under it (up to three short ones, such as "Free US address").
- **Delivery times** for air and ocean freight, in your words (for example "5 to 7 days"). They appear in the key figures and in each service story.
- **Your own key figure**, such as "$0" with "US address included". Only write what you offer.
- **Air and ocean stories**: a title, a short text and up to four promises each, shown beside your air and sea images.
- **Why choose us**: up to four short cards.
- **Closing call to action**: the last title and sentence of the page.

Write each text in English, French and Spanish with the language tabs.

## What comes from your records
- *Packages delivered*: counted from the shipments picked up or delivered, rounded down (for example "8,500+"). It shows from 500 packages. Switch it off under Sections if you prefer.
- *Pickup points*: your public locations where customers collect.
- *Rates and the estimate*: when you publish prices, the page shows your published price lists and lets visitors estimate one package by service, pickup point and weight. It is always shown as an estimate: customs, taxes and insurance are not included.

## Customer testimonials
1. Open **Your website** and choose **Customer testimonials**.
2. Enter the customer's words as they gave them, the name as they agreed to show it, and optionally the place, a rating and where they said it (Google, Facebook, at the counter).
3. Tick **The customer agreed to be quoted on our website**. Without it, the testimonial is refused. Who confirmed it, and when, is recorded.
4. Up to six shown testimonials appear on the home page, in this order. **Hide** takes one off the page; **Remove** deletes it.

## Good to know
- Testimonials are never edited: to change one, remove it and record it again with the customer's agreement.
- Customers can also review {brandName} in the customer portal. Their reviews arrive in the same list: see *Customer reviews: ask, publish or hide*.
- Preview before you publish: nothing changes for visitors until you publish.
