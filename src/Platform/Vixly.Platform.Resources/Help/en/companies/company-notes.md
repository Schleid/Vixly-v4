Internal company notes give Vixly staff context for later work on an account. Use them to record a support outcome, an agreed follow-up or a correction to an earlier note. They stay inside Control and are not messages to the company.

Notes are kept as a history. Correct a mistake by adding a new note that explains the correction. Do not expect to overwrite a previous entry, and do not put passwords, recovery codes or payment-card information in a note.

## Steps
1. Open the intended company and check its identity before entering account information.
2. Find **Internal notes**. Read the latest entries so that your note adds useful context rather than repeating an unresolved question.
3. Enter **New note**. State what happened and what remains to be done. Distinguish confirmed facts from something that still needs checking.
4. Choose **Add note**. Wait for the result, then check the new entry in the list.

## What happens next
The saved note appears with its author and time. Other staff consulting this account can use it to continue the work. Sending an email or making an account change remains a separate action; writing a note does not perform either task.
