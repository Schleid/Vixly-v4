Control is the Vixly staff workspace. It brings together company accounts, commercial agreements, subscription billing and support. Use it when your work concerns a company's relationship with Vixly.

A company's warehouse work and its customers' shipments belong to the company's own workspace. Control does not replace those screens. Check which application you need before starting a support task.

## Steps
1. Start from the home page and review the information available to your account. The cards shown depend on your access; a missing card does not mean that there is no work elsewhere.
2. Use the navigation to open the area you need. The company search in the top bar leads to the company directory.
3. Open **Help** for guidance. Search by the task you want to complete, or choose an article from a section.
4. Use the question-mark link beside the search to open help for your current page.

## What happens next
Help shows the articles appropriate to your current account. A related article may be absent when it describes a different responsibility. Reading help does not change company data, subscriptions or payments.
