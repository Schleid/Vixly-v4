Use your own Vixly staff account whenever you work in Control. Keep your password, authenticator and recovery codes private. Do not share a signed-in session with another person, even for a short support task.

Sign-in screens provide their own instructions. Complete the requested confirmation before continuing. If you cannot sign in, follow the recovery choices shown there or ask the colleague responsible for staff access.

## Steps
1. Open **My account** after signing in. Check the profile so you know you are using the intended account.
2. To change your password, enter **Current password**, **New password** and **New password again**. Choose **Change password**.
3. Review **Open sessions**. End an unfamiliar session with **End**, or use **Sign out on all other devices** to close your other sessions.
4. Select your preferred **Language of the console and your emails** and choose **Save** when you want to change the console language.

## What happens next
A password change or ended session affects access, so check the success or error message before continuing. Keep the current account page open until the action finishes. Passkeys and replacement recovery codes have their own help article.
