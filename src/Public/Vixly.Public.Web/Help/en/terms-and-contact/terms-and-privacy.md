The **Legal** page presents the Vixly documents that apply to accounts today. Use it to read the current terms and the policies included with them before accepting the sign-up form.

## Steps
1. Open **Legal** from the public site’s footer. Read the version shown for the terms and any effective date or summary provided.
2. Select **Download the Terms (PDF)** to download the terms document. Keep the document if you need a record for your company.
3. Under **Included documents**, open the documents that are listed. These can include privacy, data processing, acceptable use and service-level documents. The page identifies each included version.
4. Return to the company sign-up form when you have reviewed the applicable documents. Read **Terms of Service and Privacy Policy** and select the acceptance checkbox only if you agree.

## What happens next
Registration records acceptance of the current terms and privacy versions presented for sign-up. Downloading a document or browsing the legal page does not by itself submit that acceptance.

This help article explains where to find the documents; it does not replace their wording. Use the published PDFs for the actual conditions. If the page cannot load a document, or you need clarification before accepting, contact the team. Procedures for an existing account being asked to accept updated terms belong to that account’s workspace, rather than the public registration form.

## On this screen

1 points to downloading the current terms; 2 marks the included policies and their download links. Review the version beside each document.
